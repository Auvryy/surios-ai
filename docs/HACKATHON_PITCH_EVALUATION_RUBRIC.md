## 1. Evaluation Criteria

Evaluate each pitch using the four criteria below. Assign a rating from 1 to 5 for each criterion, then calculate its weighted score. The maximum overall score is 100 points.

## 2. Rating Scale and Calculation

| Rating | Performance Level | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **5** | **Outstanding** | Exceeds expectations; clear, compelling, and supported by strong evidence. |
| **4** | **Very Good** | Strong performance with only minor gaps. |
| **3** | **Satisfactory** | Meets basic expectations but has areas that need improvement. |
| **2** | **Needs Improvement** | Significant weaknesses or important evidence is missing. |
| **1** | **Poor** | Does not adequately demonstrate the required competency. |

**Formula:**  
$$\text{Weighted Score} = (\text{Rating} \div 5) \times \text{Criterion Weight}$$

*Example: A rating of 4 for a criterion weighted at 25 points earns $(4 \div 5) \times 25 = 20\text{ points}$.*

## 3. SDG Alignment Guide

Candidates should identify the SDG most relevant to their project and explain the connection between the problem, the proposed solution, and the intended outcome. They may identify additional SDGs when the links are credible. Do not award points simply for naming multiple goals.

| Sustainable Development Goal | Illustrative Project Relevance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SDG 1 — No Poverty** | Livelihood opportunities, financial inclusion, access to community assistance. |
| **SDG 2 — Zero Hunger** | Smart agriculture, crop disease detection, food security, agricultural forecasting. |
| **SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being** | Health monitoring, disease prevention, accessible healthcare. |
| **SDG 4 — Quality Education** | Adaptive learning, educational access, student support tools. |
| **SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation** | Water quality monitoring, sanitation access, water conservation. |
| **SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy** | Energy efficiency, renewable energy access, smart energy monitoring. |
| **SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure** | AI solutions, resilient digital infrastructure, smart industry. |
| **SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities** | Traffic management, public transport, disaster readiness, smart-city systems. |
| **SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production** | Waste reduction, recycling, resource optimization, food waste prevention. |
| **SDG 13 — Climate Action** | Climate-risk prediction, flood monitoring, early warning systems, environmental analytics. |
| **SDG 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions** | Transparent public services, access to information, accountable digital systems. |
| **SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals** | Platforms that enable collaboration, data sharing, or partnerships across sectors. |

*Note: These are examples, not a complete list. Any of the 17 UN SDGs may be relevant depending on the project. A locally focused project can demonstrate strong alignment when its contribution is specific and credible.*

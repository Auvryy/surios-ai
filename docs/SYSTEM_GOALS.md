# SuriOS — System Goals, Problem Domain & SDG 9 Alignment

This document outlines the core mission, problem domain, economic impact model, and UN SDG 9 alignment for **SuriOS**.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

**SuriOS** is an automated, AI-driven public civil infrastructure defect detection, hazard triage, and municipal maintenance dispatch platform. 

Its mission is to transform municipal road management from **reactive crisis response** (repairing massive road failures after public outcry or accidents) into **continuous, proactive, preventative maintenance** powered by crowdsourced citizen intelligence and multimodal vision AI.

---

## 2. Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goal 9

SuriOS directly advances **UN SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure**, specifically:

* **Target 9.1: Develop quality, reliable, sustainable, and resilient infrastructure:**
  * By detecting road distress at the earliest stages (micro-cracks, surface ravelling, small depressions), SuriOS prevents road structural collapse caused by subsurface water ingress.
* **Target 9.4: Upgrade infrastructure and retrofit industries to make them sustainable:**
  * Preventative maintenance reduces the volume of asphalt, heavy equipment runtime, and carbon emissions required compared to full-depth road reconstruction.
* **Civic Inclusivity & Safe Mobility:**
  * SuriOS prioritizes risks to Vulnerable Road Users (VRUs)—such as motorcycle riders, tricycle drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians—ensuring public infrastructure serves all demographics equitably.

---

## 3. The Core Municipal Challenge

Local Government Units (LGUs) and municipal engineering departments face three systemic bottlenecks:

1. **The Inspection Blind Spot:** Municipalities rely on periodic, manual road survey drives by limited engineering staff. Minor roads, suburban corridors, and barangay streets remain uninspected for months or years.
2. **The Social Media Noise Dilemma:** Citizens frequently report potholes by posting photos on Facebook, Twitter, or community groups. These complaints lack GPS metadata, standardized damage measurements, and severity classifications, making automated queuing impossible.
3. **The Triage Bottleneck:** City engineers spend hundreds of hours manually sorting through complaints, dispatching crews without knowing the exact tools, materials, or asphalt quantities required before arriving on site.

---

## 4. The Economic Impact: Preventative vs. Reactive Maintenance

In civil highway engineering, road pavement deteriorates along a non-linear lifecycle curve:

| Maintenance Stage | Distress Symptoms | Typical Repair Method | Relative Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Early Surface Distress** | Hairline cracks, minor ravelling | Crack sealing, fog seal | **1x (\$5 – \$15 / m²)** |
| **Phase 2: Moderate Distress** | Shallow potholes (<50mm), alligator cracking | Cold/hot mix patching, localized milling | **4x – 6x (\$30 – \$60 / m²)** |
| **Phase 3: Structural Failure** | Deep craters, base course displacement, subgrade saturation | Full-depth reclamation, total repaving | **10x – 25x (\$150 – \$350 / m²)** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Cost Multiplier:** Every \$1 invested in Phase 1 or early Phase 2 preventative maintenance saves municipal governments between \$4 to \$10 in emergency structural resurfacing later. SuriOS detects and triages defects before they cross into catastrophic Phase 3 failure.

## 5. Target User Personas

1. **The Daily Commuter / Citizen:**
   * Needs a fast, friction-free way to report dangerous road hazards in under 15 seconds without creating complex accounts or filling out lengthy forms.
2. **The Field City Inspector:**
   * Uses SuriOS on mobile or tablet during routine patrols to log geotagged road distress with automated civil engineering ratings.
3. **The Municipal Public Works Dispatcher:**
   * Operates the desktop operations dashboard, monitors real-time hazard clusters across city districts, and dispatches field maintenance crews with prioritized severity tickets.
4. **The Road Maintenance Crew Chief:**
   * Receives clear dispatch tickets complete with exact GPS coordinates, photo evidence, distress classification, and an AI-generated materials checklist (e.g., asphalt volume, tamping equipment).
